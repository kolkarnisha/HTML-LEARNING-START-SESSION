Why I prefer 13 steps over the current 9

Your 9-step version is perfectly valid as a high-level roadmap.

But for a beginner learning platform, 13 steps are much better because the learner can clearly understand:

What am I doing now? Why am I doing it? What should I have built before moving forward?

For example, instead of:

Step 2 — Architecture & Design

you can teach:

Step 2 — Design the Application

and actually make the learner produce:

User Flow
    ↓
Wireframes
    ↓
Pages
    ↓
Database Entities
    ↓
ER Diagram
    ↓
API List

Then the website asks:

"Have you completed these?"
